# Clean-Room Comparison — Final-Criteria Study

## Determination

The record is sufficient for the **gate-stage no-migration decision** and for
the narrower exclusions recorded below. It is not sufficient for a completed
comparison under the frozen plan or for the strategic buy/adapt/fork decision.
In particular, it cannot establish that current or reduced Vuoro is broadly
superior, nor can it award a final
`buy`, `wrap`, `remove`, `keep-bespoke-reduced`, or
`keep-bespoke-current` result for every R1–R8 boundary.

This distinction matters. A safe incumbent default follows when no candidate
has qualified to take authority. A final comparative disposition requires the
positive, comparable evidence specified by the frozen thresholds; absence of
a qualifying replacement is not that evidence.

The package is therefore closed for migration, not complete as a
whole-product or economic comparison. The migration gate may reopen only for a
qualified integration, while the strategic assessment may begin with the
source-level mapping and variant comparison described in the
[strategic assessment](strategic-assessment.md).

## Evidence-quality finding

The completed entries are deliberately narrow probes, not complete contract
runs. Their manifests preserve the run identity and participant locks, but
they do not contain the full contract ledger (`measurements.yaml`,
`adaptation.yaml`, `authority-reconciliation.yaml`, `cost-inputs.yaml`, and,
for most probes, separate fixture/candidate/event files). That is acceptable
for recording a limited exclusion, but it independently prevents promotion of
any probe to a full-scenario pass or final threshold result.

The missing ledger must not be backfilled from recollection. A future
qualifying run needs a fresh run ID, locked participant/configuration/fixture,
and the complete required evidence set before it begins.

## Finalization conditions

A future assessment may replace this gate-stage conclusion with final boundary
dispositions only when all of the following are true for a genuinely
integrated candidate:

1. The planner cannot make an authoritative execution-state transition without
   the current Restate decision/receipt, and it can make the same transition
   with that receipt.
2. The complete R2 sequence passes: concurrent acquire, proofless delegated
   mutation, transfer rotation, stale-proof rejection, controlled recovery,
   and stale post-recovery rejection.
3. The authority count identifies one owner for every disagreement state, and
   an executable reconciliation procedure succeeds. The new seam must reduce,
   not increase, reconciliation paths.
4. Comparable S-BATCH, S-SOLO, S-RESUME, and S-SIMPLIFY are complete. Seed
   S-DORMANT when the integration begins and record it only after fourteen
   days; do not fabricate an accelerated substitute.
5. The run records scenario-segmented operator time, agent/tool activity,
   retries/failures, setup, carrying work, adaptation, and rollback evidence.
   Do not average scenario classes or use the gate probes to estimate costs.
6. R6 passes end to end and the rollback invariant remains true: stopping the
   adapter rejects candidate callbacks and returns work to the retained Vuoro
   proof/revision; planner state remains a projection until reconciliation
   confirms agreement.

Until those conditions are met, the correct result is: **no migration; retain
Vuoro's authority kernel; treat planner, execution, audit, knowledge, and
projection substitutions as separate hypotheses rather than one replacement
program.**
